
# Switching-OS
# Switching From Windows 11 to Fedora KDE Plasma on a MSI Cyborg 15

I recently made a switch from **Windows 11** to **Fedora KDE Plasma**, i will be documenting my experiences and the software i have installed.

# Why i decided to switch to Fedora KDE Plasma
* I decided to switch OS because i found Windows customisation and overall control of your own devices to be very constricting and limited.
*  Moreover the amount of straight bloatware was insane, i would clear my storage monthly because windows would install useless apps which would limit my performace
*  The breaking Point was when i started learning Cybersecuity and Computer Science in College, i download **VMWare** and tried running **Parrot OS**, the amount of stuttering and crashing was insane

# Why Fedora KDE?
* After researching the best Linux Distro for my everyday life and college needs i decided on **Fedora KDE Plasma**.
* Fedora had better compatibility with my **NVIDIA GPU and Driver** over other distros like **Arch, CatchyOS and Debian**
* And was more compatible with my school work

# How i Switched OS
* I bought a **[SanDisk Ultra USB 3.0 128GB](https://www.amazon.co.uk/SanDisk-transfer-Password-protection-RescuePRO/dp/B00P8XQPY4)** for the instillation process (You dont need 128GB, only around 10 at most)
* I downloaded **[Fedora KDE Plasma Desktop 45](https://fedoraproject.org/kde/download/)** and flashed it on my usb using **[Rufus](https://rufus.ie/en/)**
* I then Booted my PC from the Boot Menus, i checked compatability with my internet and other needs using the **live mode** before the full switch.

# Issues ive come accross
* I've experienced minor issues, however all have been resolved easily
* One of them is the **NVIDIA GPU**, is that i had to enroll a signing key using **mokutill** because my **Secure Boot was enabled**
* Apart from that the only issue was forgetting my Encryption Passphase, but that was solved easily.

# Performance Changes
* I am using way less RAM storage, CPU % and all around cleaner and smoother experiance
* My laptop has not overheated once since ive been using it which is amazing
* I tried using **[Virtual Box](https://www.virtualbox.org/)** running **[Kali Linux](https://www.kali.org/)** and the performace is identical of that of my Host OS

# Software Ive installed
- **WireShark, Steam, Discord, Virtual Box, Pengiun Mail, Brave, Spotify, and Kitty as my terminal**
